A better investment choice

Purpose-built for wholesale investors seeking secured property income

All Roads Wholesale Fund gives qualifying investors exposure to Australian property development lending, with each facility assessed on security, gearing, borrower strength and a clearly defined exit.

01

Secured income focus

Investor capital is deployed into loans secured by registered first mortgages over Australian property.

02

Conservative gearing

Loan-to-value ratios are capped at 70%, maintaining a meaningful borrower equity buffer.

03

Defined investment horizon

Underlying loans are typically structured over 12-36 months, aligned to project milestones and exits.

04

Experienced governance

Taxation, legal, accounting and development expertise supports due diligence and lending decisions.

05

Direct property exposure

Investors access property-backed lending without directly owning, developing or managing property.
