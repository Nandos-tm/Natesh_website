The Fund at a glance

A focused approach to property development finance

All Roads Property Fund finances all forms of property development across Australia, with capital protected by conservative lending parameters and rigorous due diligence.

First mortgage security

Every loan is secured by a registered first mortgage over the subject property.

Up to 70% LVR

Conservative loan-to-value ratios maintain a meaningful equity buffer on each deal.

12–36 month terms

Short-to-medium loan terms aligned to clearly defined development timelines.

Australia-wide

No geographic limit, with a focus on quality Australian property development projects.
