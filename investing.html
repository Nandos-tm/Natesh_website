Overview

A disciplined way to invest in Australian property

The Fund lends to property developers across Australia, with each loan secured by a registered first mortgage over the underlying property. This structure aims to deliver investors income while preserving capital through conservative lending and rigorous due diligence.

All Roads is open to wholesale and sophisticated investors who qualify under section 761G of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), with a minimum investment of $100,000.

  • Registered first mortgage security on every loan
  • Conservative maximum 70% loan-to-value ratio
  • Loan terms from 12 months
